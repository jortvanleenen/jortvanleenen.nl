---
title: Won the GDMC AI Settlement Generation Challenge at CoG 2026
date: 2026-09-10
---

Together with Robert Salden, Lana van Sprang, Niels Versteeg, and Jasper van der Zwet, I competed as team **Merge
Conflict** in the 9th
[AI Settlement Generation Challenge (GDMC)](https://gendesignmc.wikidot.com/), part of the
[IEEE Conference on Games (CoG 2026)](https://cog2026.org) in Madrid, Spain. Our AI-driven Minecraft settlement
generator took first place, achieving the highest overall score in the competition and the highest aesthetics score
ever awarded in the challenge's history. The project was developed as part of
[Mike Preuss](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/staffmembers/mike-preuss)'s Modern Game AI course at LIACS.
